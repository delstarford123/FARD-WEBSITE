Executive Summary

FARD is a pioneering Community-Based Organization (CBO) established in Kakamega County to transform the local agricultural landscape. Formed as a strategic partnership between Delstarford Works (DSW)—experts in AI-powered software—and Farmerman Systems Group (FS)—experts in grassroots agronomy and community mobilization—FARD serves as a central hub designed to empower rural communities.

By leveraging agricultural intelligence, smart farming technologies, and robust market linkages, FARD bridges the gap between digital innovation and on-ground farming, aiming to transition smallholder farmers from subsistence farming to profitable, resilient agribusinesses.

Our Vision

To foster a thriving, resilient ecosystem for agripreneurs in Kakamega County through agricultural intelligence, smart farming technologies, and sustainable agroecology.

Our Mission

To act as an operational and strategic bridge that empowers rural communities by transitioning smallholder farmers from subsistence farming to profitable agribusinesses, leveraging data-driven market linkages and STEAM inclusion.

Our Strategic Pillars

1. Agriculture & Agribusiness

Promoting sustainable, climate-resilient farming practices that protect Kakamega's ecosystem while maximizing yields.

2. Data-Driven Market Linkage

Connecting farmer data directly to market demand, eliminating exploitative middlemen and ensuring fair pricing.

3. Market Intelligence

Providing AI-driven insights, pricing trends, and weather data to facilitate informed planting and selling decisions.

4. Precision Agriculture

Introducing technology-driven farming techniques to optimize inputs and maximize outputs.

5. STEAM Inclusion

Socioeconomic empowerment of women and youth by integrating STEAM education for the next gen of agripreneurs.

The Partnership Model

Delstarford Works (DSW)

Provides the technological backbone, leading the design and deployment of AI-powered software solutions, data infrastructure, and digital tools tailored for rural communities.

Farmerman Systems Group (FS)

Drives the grassroots execution, leading community mobilization, cooperative engagement, system implementation, and on-ground agroecology training.

Financial & Operational Model

FARD operates a highly transparent, dual-purpose financial structure designed to attract grants while sustaining technological innovation:

Non-Profit Grant Management

FARD acts as the funding vehicle for philanthropic grants, NGO partnerships, and county funds. 100% of these funds are utilized strictly for community operations, mobilization, and stipends.

The 60/40 Revenue Share

For monetized commercial projects executed jointly by DSW and FS, revenues are split: 60% remains within the CBO for community development, while 40% is distributed equally to DSW (20%) and FS (20%) for growth and technology overhead.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to join FARD?

To officially register as a member (cooperative or individual), there is a one-time non-refundable registration fee of KSh 1,000. This is followed by a monthly subscription fee of KSh 500 which supports our community initiatives and tech maintenance.

What happens if I miss my monthly subscription?

We value active participation. If subscriptions remain unpaid for 3 consecutive months, the account may be deactivated until the arrears are cleared. We encourage open communication if there are financial difficulties.

What do my fees support?

Membership fees go directly toward maintaining our AI tools, managing our circular economy projects like BSF, and providing training resources to our youth and farming communities through our 60/40 non-profit model.
