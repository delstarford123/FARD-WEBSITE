Organizational Profile

Location: Kakamega, Kenya
Status: Registered CBO
Founded: 2024

Our Essence

Bridging Digital Innovation and Grassroots Agronomy.

FARD is a pioneering Community-Based Organization established to transform the agricultural landscape of Kakamega County. We serve as a strategic hub where AI-powered technology meets sustainable community mobilization.

"Our goal is to transition smallholder farmers from subsistence farming to profitable, resilient agribusinesses through data-driven market linkages."

Our Vision

To foster a thriving, resilient ecosystem for agripreneurs in Kenya through agricultural intelligence and sustainable agroecology.

Our Mission

To act as an operational bridge that empowers rural communities by transitioning farmers to profitable agribusinesses via STEAM inclusion.

FARD is powered by the strategic synergy of two specialized entities, ensuring both high-tech development and grassroots execution.

Technological backbone: AI software, data infra, and digital tool architecture.

Our sustainable financial model ensures community growth and tech maintenance.

Transparency & FAQ

How much does it cost to join FARD? ↓
To officially register as a member (cooperative or individual), there is a one-time non-refundable registration fee of KSh 1,000, followed by a monthly subscription of KSh 500.
What happens if I miss my monthly subscription? ↓
Accounts may be deactivated after 3 consecutive months of non-payment. We encourage open communication to resolve financial difficulties before deactivation.
