Governance Model

Built for Accountability

FARD operates under a strict governance model designed for long-term sustainability and trust. Our 60/40 revenue and grant management structure ensures that the majority of commercial revenue directly supports our non-profit community initiatives.

The 60/40 Split

60% of commercial revenue from our social enterprise wings (DSW & FS) is funneled directly into community grants and training stipends.

Operational Oversight

  • Executive Committee meets monthly with a strict 50%+1 quorum requirement.

  • Financial year from Jan 1st to Dec 31st with annual external audits.

  • Open-book policy for the Kakamega Sub-County Social Development Office.
