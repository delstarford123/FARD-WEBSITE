Governance Model

FARD operates under a strict governance model designed for accountability. Our 60/40 revenue and grant management structure ensures that 60% of commercial revenue directly supports our non-profit community initiatives, creating a self-sustaining cycle of development.

Governance & Operations

FARD maintains strict operational oversight. Our Executive Committee meets monthly (requiring a 50%+1 quorum for binding resolutions), and our financial year runs from January 1st to December 31st. Our books are open for audit by the Kakamega Sub-County Social Development Office.
