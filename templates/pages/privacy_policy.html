Privacy Policy

Your data is the seed of our innovation. We protect it with enterprise-grade security and absolute transparency.

01 Data Collection & AI Usage

FARD collects data necessary for precision agriculture, including soil health metrics, crop images, and cooperative membership details. This data is used to train our AI models to provide better diagnosis and yield predictions. We never sell individual farmer data to third parties.

02 Data Sovereignty

We believe in the principle of data sovereignty. Farmers and cooperatives retain ownership of their primary data. You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your information from our systems at any time.

03 Security Infrastructure

All data is stored using high-availability cloud infrastructure with end-to-end encryption. Our partnership with Delstarford Works ensures that our technological backbone meets international security standards.
